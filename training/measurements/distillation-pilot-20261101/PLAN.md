# Small evaluator + more MCTS: distillation pilot

Prepare while the optimized transformer12h run continues; do not modify its uploaded source or interrupt it. Run only after its final checkpoint/matches/backups are secured, using the same isolated L4.

Teacher: frozen ResNet13h checkpoint, already copied into the experiment. Positions: final optimized-transformer replay600k rows, containing realistic self-play states. This tests teacher imitation on that state distribution, not a replay generated by the teacher. Exact duplicate board states stay within train/validation splits; symmetry equivalents may cross splits. Separate heldout opening matches determine strength.

Three20-minute training trials with effective batch512 and seed1:

1. Transformer128wide,2blocks,2heads,expansion2: teacher distillation, hybrid replay target weight0.1.
2. ResNet64channels,4blocks: same distillation recipe.
3. Transformer128×2 control: teacher weight0, replay policy/outcome weight1.

Teacher soft logits at temperature2 plus teacher value, using T²-scaled KL and value MSE; frozen teacher and AdamW student. Cosine LR1e-3to5e-5 with200-update warmup. Time budgets include online teacher forward passes and student updates; initialization/validation excluded and reported separately. No additional self-play or claims of higher eventual ceiling.

Evaluate inference and paired-color strength at equal200visits, then equal target20msper move (100paired openings,200games for each protocol). Timed matches play games sequentially, with two resident engines and independent retained trees/caches. Warm batch-one inference first; include features, history, search and backups in the move clock; exclude model setup and move advancement. Stop at a completed-wave boundary with at least one visited child, record actual mean/p95/max times and new evaluations per side. Report overruns; do not claim exact hard deadlines. Include the replay-only control to distinguish imitation benefits from supervised warmup. The timing extension compiles a separate az-timed library and does not modify the training engine.

Candidate speed is not inferred from parameter count. Small ResNet is included because it may offer the better strength/latency trade-off. Strongest checkpoint at equal visits is not automatically strongest under equal compute.
